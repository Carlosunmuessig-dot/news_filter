Present Perfect vs. Simple Past

Das Present Perfect beschreibt Handlungen mit Bezug zur Gegenwart. Simple Past ist für abgeschlossene Handlungen in der Vergangenheit.
❌ Falsch: "I have seen him yesterday." → Zeitadverb "yesterday" erfordert Simple Past.
✅ Richtig: "I saw him yesterday." / "I have already seen the movie."

If-Clauses (Conditionals)

Type I: reale Bedingung (If + Present Simple → will + Infinitiv). Type II: irreale Bedingung Gegenwart (If + Past Simple → would + Infinitiv).
❌ Falsch: "If I would have time, I would help." → "would" gehört nicht in den if-Satz.
✅ Richtig: "If I had time, I would help."

Since vs. For

Since = Zeitpunkt (since Monday, since 2020). For = Zeitraum (for 3 days, for a long time).
❌ Falsch: "I've lived here since 5 years."
✅ Richtig: "I've lived here for 5 years." / "I've lived here since 2019."
