Anti-Germanismen & Elite Expressions

1. The "Information" Trap

In German, "Informationen" is plural. In English, "information" is strictly uncountable.

We need more informations about the deal.
We need more information regarding the transaction.

2. Executive Transitions

Avoid basic connector words. Elevate your transitions for business writing.

But we have to cut costs.
However, cost optimization remains a priority.
Nevertheless, capital preservation is paramount.

3. The "Make" vs "Do" Fallacy

Germans often say "make a decision" (eine Entscheidung machen - falsch!). Wait, actually "make a decision" is correct in English! But they say "make an experience" instead of "have an experience".

We made a good experience with them.
We had a positive experience working with them.
